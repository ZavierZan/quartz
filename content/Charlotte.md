## Background

Graphic designer, quiet loner, insomnia sufferer  

Charlotte was born in Townia, raised by her grandmother in the northernmost house of the town. Her parents left when she was a baby — no one talks about them much. When she was 9, she left with her grandmother to the city after a _“strange accident”_ at the quarry. She’s never remembered what happened — only that there was fog.

She hasn’t returned since.  
Until now.

---

## Recent Activity

### **The Nightmares Begin**

Months ago, Charlotte began having vivid, repeating nightmares:

- Always set in a fog-covered version of Townia
- Always following a child’s voice she never sees
- Always ending with a massive eye opening beneath her

The dreams worsened — insomnia followed, and her grip on reality began to fray. Her last sketch at work was of a lighthouse she didn’t remember drawing.

She finally gave in and returned to Townia — desperate for answers, or peace.
Arrived to town
